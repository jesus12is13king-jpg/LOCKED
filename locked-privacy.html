Privacy Policy

LOCKED ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what we collect, why, and how you can control it. LOCKED is designed on the principle that your habit data is yours — not a product we sell.

1. What we collect

LOCKED collects the minimum data needed to function:

  • Account data: an email address (or anonymous identifier) used solely to create and secure your account.
  • Habit data: the habits you log, their dates, and related activity/spending notes. This lives primarily on your device.
  • Subscription status: whether you are on a free or Plus plan, so we can unlock the correct features.
  • Referral data: counts of referrals you've made, to support the free-Plus unlock.

2. How we use it

  • To provide core app functionality (tracking your habits).
  • To manage your account and subscription.
  • To grant Plus access via referrals or purchases.
  • We do not sell your data, show third-party ads, or use your data for profiling beyond the features above.

3. Purchases & subscriptions

Plus subscriptions are processed by Google Play (Android) — and in future by Apple (iOS) — through our billing partner RevenueCat. Payment details are handled entirely by the platform store; LOCKED never sees your card number.

4. Data storage & security

Account and referral data is stored with Supabase, our backend provider, encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest. Habit data is stored locally on your device and synced only as needed for your account.

5. Third-party services

We use the following processors to operate the service: Supabase (backend/account storage) and RevenueCat (subscription management). These providers are bound by their own privacy and security commitments.

6. Data sharing

We do not share your personal data with third parties for their own marketing. We share only the minimum with the processors above to operate the app.

7. Your rights

  • Access: request a copy of your data.
  • Deletion: delete your account and associated data at any time.
  • Correction: update or correct your information.
  • Portability: export your data.

To exercise any of these, contact hello@locked.app.

8. Children

LOCKED is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date above reflects the latest revision. We'll notify you of material changes within the app.

10. Contact

Questions about privacy? Email hello@locked.app.
